# Roles and review

Honor the user's model and role choices and the repository's applicable workflow. Otherwise choose from supported runtime settings according to the assignment's uncertainty and consequences. Use focused context for a fresh worker, including the constraints it would lose without the parent's history. Keep coordination with the current primary agent rather than creating another management layer to match a preferred model.

Bounded retrieval and mechanical edits can use a less costly capable worker. Consequential implementation and integration need adequate reasoning capability. Distinguish a task that exceeds the worker's capability from one that lacks a decision, context, or authority before escalating. Avoid assuming that maximum effort improves every assignment. A specific provider model mapping belongs in user or project configuration, not a universal skill default.

## Independent review

Use independent review when a substantial completed slice or a concrete unresolved risk warrants it. Routine changes do not require another agent. Known implementation failures should be corrected before reviewing the integrated result unless the investigation of that failure is itself the assignment.

Give a fresh reviewer the requested behavior, exact source or diff, relevant callers, known limits, and check evidence. Keep it read-only unless another role was authorized. Do not prime it with the implementer's conclusions. Ask for actionable defects with locations, consequences, and evidence, plus material verification gaps.

The coordinator assesses the findings, assigns corrections, and verifies the affected behavior. A reviewer response is scoped evidence, not certification or permission to publish. Repeat review only for a concern or source change that makes the earlier conclusion insufficient.
