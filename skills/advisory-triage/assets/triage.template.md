## Prior work and changes

Record earlier assessments, relevant discussion, and what changed. A missing local manifest does not mean the report is new. Preserve previous conclusions in checkpoints before reassessment.

## Claims and product contract

For each claim, explain the actor's existing authority, controlled input, supported configuration and release-era contract, intended capability, failed guarantee or defeating guard, additional protected impact, counterevidence, and remaining proof gaps. Link evidence to exact revisions. Explain why this is an advisory or another category, not merely whether behavior occurs.

## Duplicate coverage

Compare cause, guarantee, scope, and remediation for each claim. Link the canonical report and relevant PR. Record its live state, fix status, and owner. A closed, unfixed canonical without an owner requires a maintainer decision. Record material new value separately; exact repeats do not require credit or canonical edits.

## Branches, versions, and severity

Record exact current SHAs of the stable branch and any pre-release branch, inspected SHAs, introduction/fix/release/report chronology, supported versions, and gaps. Do not treat a remote SHA as proof that its source was inspected. Separate CVSS Base from configuration prevalence and operational priority.

## Decision and next action

Explain each claim's verdict and disposition, the report-level recommendation, owner, holds, and specific remaining decisions. Record action-specific approvals and verified results separately from recommendations.

## Reporter response

Include ready-to-post Markdown and the advisory URL. Duplicate replies link the canonical advisory and state that it stays private until publication. Do not post automatically.

## Handoff

Identify the approved shared location and audience, checkpoint revision, unresolved work, evidence links and their access constraints, and the next action. A local checkpoint is not a shared handoff. The next maintainer must verify the record against live GitHub discussion, metadata, and current refs before acting.
